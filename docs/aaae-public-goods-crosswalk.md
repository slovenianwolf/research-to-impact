# Research-to-Impact — Public Goods × AAAE Crosswalk

A working map of the public goods committed under the Ignite grant (Bill & Melinda
Gates Foundation), aligned to the foundation's **AAAE** framework — **A**vailability,
**A**wareness, **A**doption, **E**fficacy — and to where each good lives on the
public site (researchtoimpact.marshallstreet.org).

Purpose: a single page to (a) confirm we have a home for every committed output,
and (b) report dissemination against AAAE. Source of truth for the commitments is
the Ignite Investment Document (Project Description, Investment Results — Outcomes
and Outputs, and Global Access).

**Status legend:** ✅ built · 🟡 structured, content pending · ⬜ not yet started

## How AAAE maps to the site

| AAAE stage | What it means here | Where it mostly lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Availability** | The goods exist and are free to access | Practice Toolkits; Leading Implementation |
| **Awareness** | The field knows the work exists | Stories & Spotlights; homepage; signup; talks |
| **Adoption** | Schools actually put the practices to use | Toolkits + training, templates, coaching tools |
| **Efficacy** | Evidence the practices work | Evidence & Impact (evaluation, reports, case studies) |

## Design principles (why it's laid out this way)

1. **Practitioner-first.** Only the practice toolkits and Leading Implementation
   appear as homepage cards. Research and funder-facing goods (white papers,
   evaluation, annual reports, roadmap) live in a quiet **Evidence & Impact** cluster
   near About — available and discoverable, never centered. This keeps the front
   door focused on what a teacher or leader can use on Monday.

2. **Open by default.** Everything is free. Only the research tier carries an
   *optional* email ask, and even there a "no thanks, just download" path always
   works. This keeps us aligned with the foundation's **Global Access** requirement
   that funded developments be promptly, broadly, and affordably available.

3. **Sheet-driven.** Placement (`tier`, `header_label`, `order`), prominence
   (`focal`), visibility (`nav_visible`), and the email ask (`gated`) are all columns
   in the master Google Sheet. New public goods get a home by adding a row — no code
   change.

## Global Access — license

The site is licensed **CC BY 4.0** (attribution only). This was chosen to align with
the grant's Global Access requirement that funded developments be broadly
disseminated, adaptable to diverse educational contexts, and embeddable by field
partners — uses that the more restrictive CC BY-NC-ND (No-Derivatives,
Non-Commercial) would have blocked. CC BY is also the foundation's standard for
funded public goods under its Open Access policy.
